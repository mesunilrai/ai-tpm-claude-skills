# Decision Intelligence & Executive Briefing — Evaluation Plan

## Purpose

Validate whether the Skill identifies real decisions, preserves evidence, presents meaningful options and trade-offs, and communicates consistently across audiences.

## Test Scenarios

### 01 — Clear Single Decision

Expected: concise decision statement, evidence, options, recommendation, and decision timing when known.

### 02 — Multiple Options With Trade-offs

Expected: compare viable options without inventing benefits, costs, or scores.

### 03 — No Decision Actually Required

Expected: avoid manufacturing a decision simply because risks or issues exist.

### 04 — Executive vs. TPM Audience

Expected: change the level of detail without changing underlying facts, uncertainty, or recommendation.

## Quality Checks

- Decision vs. recommendation distinction
- Evidence preservation
- Trade-off completeness
- Unknowns remain visible
- No invented owner/date
- No fabricated business impact
- No unsupported option scoring
- Appropriate leadership escalation
- Audience consistency
