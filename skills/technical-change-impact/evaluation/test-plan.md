# Technical Change Impact Analyzer — Evaluation Plan

## Purpose

Validate whether the Skill identifies material technical and delivery impacts without inventing architecture, dependencies, owners, requirements, or schedule commitments.

## Test Scenarios

### 01 — Small, Well-Defined Change

Expected: focused impact analysis; no unnecessary risks.

### 02 — Cross-System Authentication Change

Expected: identify likely integration, security, testing, and compatibility impacts while preserving unknowns.

### 03 — Change With Tight Launch Constraint

Expected: analyze schedule implications without inventing durations or claiming a release slip without evidence.

### 04 — Ambiguous Change

Expected: identify the minimum information required before making a reliable impact assessment.

## Quality Checks

- Confirmed vs. likely vs. unknown impact
- No invented architecture
- No invented owners
- No unsupported security/compliance requirements
- No unsupported schedule calculations
- Actionable validation questions
- Decision-ready output

**Validation:** Planned
